02 · B2B

B2B Studies

B2B means Business to Business. Respondents are professionals who make or influence decisions at their companies.

IT Decision Makers

CIOs, IT Managers and technology decision makers.

Business Decision Makers

CEO, VP, Director and other business leaders.

SME / Experts

Subject-matter specialists such as cybersecurity or HR experts.

Industry Managers

Managers and above across operations, procurement, insurance, construction and more.

03 · B2C

B2C Studies

B2C means Business to Consumer. These studies target everyday people based on demographics, income, behavior or purchasing activity.

👪 General Population

Broad consumer opinions, commonly adults 18+.

💵 Income Based

Household-income targeting for relevant products.

📺 Behavior Based

Streaming users, travelers, shoppers and category users.

🎓 Parents / Shoppers

Specific consumer groups for education, retail and CPG studies.

04 · Healthcare

Healthcare Studies

Healthcare research can involve medical professionals, patients and caregivers. Accuracy and eligibility are especially important.

🩺 HCPs

Doctors, specialists, nurses, pharmacists, physiotherapists and healthcare administrators.

🤝 Patients & Caregivers

Relevant patient populations and caregivers providing experience-based feedback.

10 · Rejections

Four Main Rejection Categories

🤖

Fraudulent Behavior

Duplicates, bots, multiple accounts and other invalid activity.

😴

Quality & Engagement

Speeding, straight-lining and contradictions.

💬

Open-End Issues

Gibberish, one-word or irrelevant responses.

📋

Screening Failures

False eligibility, quota mismatch or location inconsistency.

11 · Fraud

Fraudulent Behavior

  • Duplicate responses: repeated entries can compromise data quality.
  • Automated/bot responses: unnatural automated completion is invalid.
  • Survey farming: unusually high-volume activity can trigger quality controls.
  • Location misuse: misrepresenting geography can invalidate a response.

Professional standard: use only legitimate, approved testing procedures and never misrepresent a respondent.

12 · Quality

Quality & Engagement Issues

⚡

Speeding

Completing far faster than the expected survey duration may indicate poor engagement.

➡️

Straight-Lining

Selecting the same rating for every grid item without genuine consideration.

⚠️

Inconsistent Answers

Answers must make logical sense together.

👀

Attention Checks

Read instructions carefully and follow explicit attention-check directions.

13 · Open Ends

Open-Ended Answer Quality

Open-ended questions are reviewed for genuine engagement and relevance.

Do

Write 1–3 natural, specific sentences and address the actual topic.

Avoid

Gibberish, irrelevant text, one-word answers and repeated copy-paste responses.

14 · Screening

Screening & Advanced Flags

🎭

False Screening

Claiming a role or experience that does not match verification questions.

📍

Geo / Location Mismatch

Stated location and verified location do not agree.

🧠

Impossible Answers

Age, experience and job level should remain logically possible.

🐠

Red Herring Brand

Made-up brands may be used to detect overclaiming familiarity.

15 · Consistency

Consistency Is the Core Rule

A high-quality response is internally coherent.

Role ↔ Department

IT Manager → Information Technology.

Employees ↔ Revenue

Company size and revenue should fit the same business profile.

Age ↔ Experience

Experience claims must be realistic for the stated age.

Location ↔ Study

Eligibility must align with the study's actual requirements.

16 · Golden Rules

Day 2 Golden Rules

  1. Know the study type.
  2. Match company details logically.
  3. Use the correct job-title level.
  4. Do not rush; respect the survey's expected duration.
  5. Write genuine, on-topic open-ended responses.
  6. Read every question and attention check carefully.
17 · Practical Example

Example: 25 Employees + $3M Revenue

Assessment

25 employees fall in the Small tier (11–50). $3M annual revenue also falls in the Small tier ($1M–$10M).

Therefore, the two company-size attributes are logically aligned for this example.
